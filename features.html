Features

The whole club year,
in one place.

From the first registration in September to certificates at investiture, TrailMark follows the way Adventist clubs actually run — so the tool gets out of your way.

Investiture tracking

Every requirement, every level.

Friend, Companion, Explorer, Ranger, Voyager and Guide — each level's requirements are built in and grouped by track. Tick them off as kids complete them and the progress rolls up automatically.

  • Per-track progress so you see strengths and gaps
  • A split bar that goes gold past 100% for Advanced work
  • "Ready to Invest" flags the moment a level is finished

And the finishing touches

The little things that save the most time.

Certificates

Generate clean investiture certificates for every child who's ready — no template wrangling.

Director reports

Roster, investiture and standing reports your conference will recognize, ready to export.

Your data, yours

Export your full club records any time. No lock-in, ever — it's your ministry's data.

Works on the device you have

Built for the church foyer and the campsite alike — it runs in any browser on your phone, tablet or laptop, with no app to install.

Calm, on-brand design

A warm, uncluttered interface that respects the seriousness of ministry — and is easy enough for a brand-new volunteer to pick up.
