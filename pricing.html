Questions

Pricing, answered.

How do I know which plan I'm on?
It's based purely on how many Pathfinders are on your active roster. 25 or fewer is Small Club; 26 or more is Standard. If your club grows past 25 mid-year, we'll let you know — there's no surprise charge.
Is there a monthly option?
Right now plans are annual — it keeps the price low and matches the rhythm of a club year. A conference plan can be arranged differently; just reach out.
What happens to my data if I stop paying?
Nothing is deleted. Your account becomes read-only — you can still view and export everything. Renew whenever you like and pick up exactly where you left off.
Do you offer a discount for small or new clubs?
The Small Club tier already keeps it affordable, and the 30-day trial is free. If cost is a genuine barrier for your club, talk to us — ministry comes first.
Can a conference pay for all its clubs?
Yes — that's exactly what the Conference / Union plan is for. One license, volume pricing, and conference-wide reporting. Contact us for a quote.
