FAQ

Good questions.

Everything directors usually ask before they start. Don't see yours? Just ask us.

Getting started
What exactly is TrailMark PCM?
It's a web-based club manager for Seventh-day Adventist Pathfinder leaders. PCM stands for Pathfinder Club Manager. It keeps your roster, investiture progress, honors, attendance, points, staff and background-check tracking in one place — replacing the pile of spreadsheets and paper record cards most clubs juggle today.
Do I need to install anything?
No. TrailMark runs in any modern web browser on your phone, tablet, or computer. There's nothing to download and nothing to keep updated — just sign in and go.
How long does setup take?
Most directors have their club up and running in an afternoon. Add your Pathfinders, set their levels, and you're tracking investiture the same day. You can invite staff to help fill in the rest.
Is it hard for a new volunteer to learn?
We designed it for busy volunteers, not software experts. If you can use a messaging app, you can use TrailMark. The layout follows the way clubs already work, so most of it is self-explanatory.
Pricing & access
What will it cost?
TrailMark is launching soon, and we're partnering directly with conferences on licensing — so there's no public pricing yet. Get in touch and we'll share the details for your conference or club.
How do I get access?
We're rolling out access as we launch, starting with conferences we partner with directly. Join the early-access list and we'll reach out the moment your program is ready.
Programs & features
Which investiture levels are supported?
All six: Friend, Companion, Explorer, Ranger, Voyager and Guide — including Advanced work, which shows as a gold over-100% segment on the progress bar.
Do you support Adventurer or Master Guide clubs?
Not yet — but both are on the way as separate products. Adventurer Club Manager (grades 1–4) and Master Guide Manager are in active design. Add your email on the roadmap and you'll be first to know when they open.
Can multiple staff use the same club?
Absolutely. Invite your whole committee by email — every plan includes unlimited staff accounts, so counselors and unit leaders can keep their own areas current.
Does it handle background-check tracking?
Yes. Each staff member's Verified-Volunteer and background-check status lives on the roster, with clear flags for what's current and what's missing or expiring — so you know who's cleared to serve before camporee, not after.
Data & privacy
Who owns my club's data?
You do. We're a steward of your information, not its owner. You can export your complete records at any time, and we'll never sell or share your members' data.
Is children's information kept secure?
Protecting minors' information is something we take seriously. Access is limited to your invited staff, data is transmitted securely, and sensitive fields like family and medical notes are only visible to those who need them.
Are you affiliated with the church officially?
TrailMark is an independent tool built by club volunteers. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the General Conference of Seventh-day Adventists or its divisions. We follow the official program requirements closely, but we're not an official church product.
