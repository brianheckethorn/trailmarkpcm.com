Product updates

What's new in TrailMark.

TrailMark is in active development ahead of its conference launch. Here's what we've been building — new programs, tools for conferences, and the safeguards that protect your members' data.

June 2026
Reporting, roles & privacy
New

Conference Secretary role

Conference directors can now invite a read-only assistant to help pull and print reports — without the ability to distribute licenses or manage churches.

New

Adventurer conference reporting · Adventurers

Adventurer clubs can send a bi-monthly report up to their conference — matching the way Pathfinder clubs report each month, so the whole field reports the same way.

New

Printable certificates for every program

Generate personalized certificates for Adventurers, Pathfinders, and Master Guide, each with its own title and signature lines — ready to print or save as a PDF.

Security & privacy

Stronger family & medical privacy controls

Added role-based controls so a child's family, medical, and emergency details are shown only to the caregiving roles that need them — enforced server-side, not just hidden on screen.

Improved

Parent portal

Parents can view their own child's progress, honors earned, and the club schedule, and keep family and medical information up to date — seeing only their own child, never the roster.

Security & privacy

Account security & activity trail

Two-factor authentication for conference and administrator accounts, plus an append-only record of sensitive changes for accountability.

Earlier in 2026
Platform foundations
The core of TrailMark came together over the first half of 2026 — three complete programs and the conference layer that ties them together.
New

The Adventurers program · Ages 4–9

A bright, playful home for the littlest members: six classes with star progress, colorful awards and chips, per-class requirements, and a kid-facing "adventure map."

New

Master Guide · Ages 16+ & leaders

A regal leadership path — candidates progress through trials with mentor sign-off, toward investiture and a certificate.

New

Conference Portal & dashboard

A live view of every licensed church, a monthly-report inbox so reports arrive automatically, and youth counts from a single club up to the whole field.

New

One license, all three programs

A single church license unlocks Adventurers, Pathfinders, and Master Guide together — nothing to buy à la carte.

New

Monthly conference report · Pathfinders

The monthly conference report is generated for you from the club's own records — no more piecing it together by hand at month's end.

Improved

Conference directory & bilingual app

A built-in directory of North American Division conferences, and full English / Español support throughout the app.
