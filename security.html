Security & Privacy Overview

Trusted with your
kids' information.

Youth ministry runs on trust — and TrailMark holds some of the most sensitive data a church keeps: children's names, birthdates, medical and allergy notes, and emergency contacts. This page explains, in plain language, how we protect it and how we're building toward a formal SOC 2 posture.

How we protect your data

Security built in, not bolted on.

TrailMark is architected so that the right people see the right data — and no one else — enforced by the database itself, not just the screen.

Each club's data is walled off

Every church's roster is isolated at the database level. One club can never read another club's data — tenant separation is enforced by row-level security on every request.

Right people, right access

Clear roles from conference down to program director. Each leader sees what their role needs and nothing more — and those limits are checked on the server, so they hold even outside the app screen.

Encrypted in transit & at rest

All traffic is served over HTTPS, and data is encrypted at rest by our cloud database provider. Sensitive credentials and API keys are never exposed to the browser.

Extra sign-in security for leaders

Accounts with the broadest reach — conference and platform administrators — are prompted to enable two-factor authentication, so a password alone can't open the field-wide view.

Sensitive changes are logged

Edits to children's medical, guardian, and emergency information are recorded to an append-only trail — capturing who changed what and when, without ever storing the values themselves.

Family & medical, need-to-know

Family, medical, and emergency details are shown only to the caregiving roles that need them. Finance- and support-only roles work with the roster without ever seeing a child's medical record.

Privacy & children

Data minimization, by design.

We collect only what a club needs to run its program, and we share the least necessary at every level. Children's data is never sold, and never used for advertising or profiling.

  • Parents see only their own child. The parent portal returns a family's own child record — never the club roster or any other family's information.
  • Conferences see counts, not medical. Oversight dashboards and reports receive membership totals and progress summaries — not children's medical, contact, or birthdate details.
  • Consent is captured & recorded. Parent registration records the exact policy and terms version a parent agreed to, with a durable, tamper-resistant consent record.
  • Families can access & delete. Parents can review and update their child's information and request deletion of their data.
  • Your records, exportable. A club's data belongs to the ministry that entered it and can be exported at any time.
  • No trackers, no ad networks. TrailMark does not embed advertising trackers or sell personal data — from anyone, child or adult.

TrailMark supports clubs in meeting their obligations under children's-privacy laws such as COPPA. This overview describes our practices and is not legal advice; a club's specific obligations should be confirmed with its own counsel.

Reliability & infrastructure

Run on trusted foundations.

TrailMark is built on established, security-conscious cloud providers — not a homemade server in someone's closet.

Daily backups, restore-tested

The database is backed up automatically every day, and we periodically verify that a backup actually restores — so the safety net is real, not theoretical.

Bot & abuse protection

Sign-up and sign-in are protected against automated abuse, and dependencies are continuously scanned for known vulnerabilities.

Independent, reviewed changes

Changes that touch access, roles, or child data go through an adversarial review before release, and security-relevant work is tracked against a written readiness program.

SOC 2 — readiness in progress

Where we are on SOC 2.

TrailMark is being built toward SOC 2 readiness across the Security, Confidentiality, and Privacy trust-services criteria. We maintain a written readiness program — a control register, data inventory, access-control model, and operational policies — that guides how we design and ship.

To be clear and honest: TrailMark is not yet SOC 2 audited or certified, and we don't claim to be. A SOC 2 report can only be issued by an independent auditor. This page describes the controls that are in place today and the direction we're building — we're happy to walk your team through it in detail.
