How it works

Licensed by the conference. Run by the church.

Conferences license their churches, and each church gets the whole platform — with a simple chain of oversight from the conference down to each program.

1

Conference

Licenses its churches and watches the whole field from the Conference Portal.

2

Church

Gets the full platform — both programs — the moment it's licensed.

3

Administrative Director

One per church — sets up the club and invites each program's directors.

4

Program Directors

Adventurer & Pathfinder leaders track their own members day to day.

One license, one setup — and every program in the church is covered.
