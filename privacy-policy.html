Privacy Policy

How TrailMark handles
your family's information.

TrailMark PCM holds some of the most sensitive records a church keeps — children's names, birthdates, medical and allergy notes, and emergency contacts. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices families and clubs have.

1 Who we are

TrailMark PCM ("TrailMark," "we," "us") is a club-management web application for Seventh-day Adventist youth ministries — Adventurers, Pathfinders, and Master Guide. Clubs and their conferences use TrailMark to manage rosters, track progress and honors, coordinate staff, produce reports, and register children through their parents and guardians.

2 Our role & yours

TrailMark is provided to clubs and conferences — they are our customers. For the child and family records kept in TrailMark, the club is the organization responsible for those records and decides how they are used; TrailMark acts as the service provider that stores and processes the data on the club's behalf.

Parents and guardians interact with TrailMark through a registration portal and a limited family view of their own child. Club staff and administrators interact with TrailMark to run their program.

3 Information we collect

We collect only the information a club needs to run its youth-ministry program safely. In summary:

We do not collect payment-card data (online billing is not currently active; if it is reactivated, payments would be handled by a payment processor and card details would not be stored by TrailMark). We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal information.

4 Why we collect it

We use this information to operate the club and provide the service:

  • Maintain rosters and track youth-ministry progress, honors, and awards.
  • Help keep children safe at meetings and activities (emergency and medical information).
  • Enable parent registration, consent capture, and family communication.
  • Produce the membership and progress reports conferences need for oversight.
  • Operate, secure, back up, and support the service.

We practice data minimization — we collect and expose only what these purposes require, and no more.

5 Who can access the data

Access is enforced by the database itself — through row-level security and role-based permissions — not merely hidden on a screen. In practice:

  • One club cannot see another club's data. Each ministry's records are isolated at the database level.
  • Staff see what their role needs. Roles run from conference oversight down to program director, counselor, and support roles; family, medical, and emergency details are shown only to the caregiving roles that need them.
  • Conferences see counts, not medical. Oversight dashboards and reports receive membership totals and progress summaries — not children's medical, contact, or birthdate details.
  • Parents see only their own child. The family view returns a parent's own child record — never the club roster or any other family's information.

Sensitive administrative actions — such as edits to a child's medical, guardian, or emergency information — are recorded to an append-only audit trail that captures who changed what and when, without storing the values themselves.

7 Children's data & parental consent

TrailMark stores personal information about children on behalf of clubs. Where children under 13 are involved, we support verifiable parental consent through the registration flow and keep a durable, tamper-resistant consent record.

At registration, a parent reviews the applicable privacy notice and terms and submits their child's information — and that act records the exact policy and terms version agreed to, along with the date, time, and originating request details. This consent evidence is preserved even if the parent account is later removed.

We treat children's medical, allergy, and emergency-contact information as highly sensitive. We do not use children's data for advertising or profiling, and we do not record the values of sensitive fields in our system logs.

For clubs: TrailMark provides the tools to help your ministry meet its obligations under children's-privacy laws such as COPPA. Your specific obligations depend on your situation and should be confirmed with your own counsel.

8 Data retention

We retain a child's and family's records while they are active in the club and in the club's season archives, so a ministry keeps its history year over year. Consent records are retained as evidence of the consent given, even after an account is removed.

A club can export or remove its own data. Formal retention periods and the deletion-fulfillment workflow are being finalized and will be reflected here before this policy takes effect.

9 Your rights & choices

Families. Through the parent portal, a parent or guardian can review and update their own child's registration information and request deletion of their account and their child's associated data.

Clubs. The ministry that entered a club's data owns that data and can export it at any time.

To make a request or ask a question about your information, contact us at privacy@trailmarkpcm.com. Because clubs are responsible for their own records, some requests may be directed to, or coordinated with, the relevant club or conference.

10 How we protect it

We protect your information with a layered set of safeguards, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encryption at rest by our cloud database provider.
  • Database-enforced tenant isolation and least-privilege, role-based access.
  • Two-factor authentication, available for accounts and prompted for the broadest-reach administrators.
  • An append-only audit trail for sensitive administrative changes.
  • Secrets and API keys kept out of the browser and out of source control.
  • Daily backups with periodic restore verification, and continuous dependency scanning.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work continuously to protect your data. TrailMark is being built toward a future independent SOC 2 readiness posture; it is not yet SOC 2 audited or certified, and we do not claim to be. You can read more in our Security & Privacy overview.

11 Where data is stored

TrailMark's database and backups are hosted in the United States. If you access TrailMark from outside the United States, your information will be processed in the United States.

12 Changes to this policy

When this policy changes materially, we will update the version and, where appropriate, communicate the change through the app or the registration flow. The version a parent agreed to at registration is recorded with their consent, so it is always clear which terms applied.

13 Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email privacy@trailmarkpcm.com. We're glad to help — and to walk conferences and churches through our practices in detail.
