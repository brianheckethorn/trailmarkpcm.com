Our story

From a binder
to a better way.

TrailMark didn't start in a boardroom. It started with a stack of record cards left over from that night's meeting, and the quiet thought: there has to be a better way.

Every club has that one person who stays late after the meeting, squinting at a spreadsheet, trying to remember whether Caleb finished his knot-tying requirement.

For us, that person was a volunteer Pathfinder director — juggling a day job, a family, and a club of energetic kids who deserved someone present, not someone buried in admin. The ministry was the joy. The paperwork was the tax.

The tools that existed were either built for a different era, locked behind clunky logins, or simply didn't understand how an Adventist club actually works — the six investiture levels, the honors, the points, the background checks that keep kids safe. So we built the thing we wished we had: one calm, modern place for the whole club.

We started with Pathfinders because it's the program closest to our hearts. We're growing toward Adventurers and Master Guides because directors kept asking. And we keep it affordable because this is ministry first — a tool in service of the mission, never the other way around.

The point was never the paperwork. It's the kids.

If TrailMark gives you back even one evening a week to spend on ministry instead of admin, it's doing its job.

What we believe

The principles behind every decision.

Ministry first

Every feature has to earn its place by giving time back to the people doing the work. If it doesn't serve the mission, it doesn't ship.

Safety isn't optional

Child protection is built in, not bolted on. Background-check tracking is a first-class part of the roster, because it has to be.

Your data is yours

No lock-in, ever. Export your full records any time. We're a steward of your ministry's information, not its owner.
